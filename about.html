OUR ETHOS & ORIGIN

A studio built to make good grooming easy.

FADE STUDIO was founded on a simple realization: visiting a salon or barber shouldn't be an intimidating gamble or a rushed chore. It should be a restorative ritual led by artists who listen first.

Meet the Specialists Our Core Values
THE BACKGROUND

Born from respect for the craft.

For too long, grooming has been split into two extremes: noisy, chaotic neighbourhood shops that rush you in fifteen minutes, or overpriced luxury salons that prioritize pretension over precision.

In 2024, Vikram Malhotra and Priya Sharma founded FADE STUDIO in Bengaluru to establish a thoughtful middle ground. A serene, minimalist sanctuary where men can get master-level skin fades and straight-razor shaves, and women can receive contemporary haircuts and bespoke colour treatments without attitude.

We view grooming as an essential form of self-respect. When your hair is cut right and your skin is cared for, you carry yourself differently. That quiet confidence is what we build every day.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The three pillars of FADE STUDIO.

We measure our success not by how many chairs we turn over, but by the trust of our returning clients.

01

Listen First

We never pick up the clippers until we understand what you want. We discuss cowlicks, lifestyle, maintenance preferences, and how you style your hair at home.

02

Clean Work

Surgical hygiene is non-negotiable. Medical-grade UV sterilization for all tools, fresh sanitized capes for every guest, single-use razor blades, and pristine stations.

03

Fair Prices

Transparent pricing with no surprise add-ons. Haircuts include wash and finish. Beard care includes hot towel compresses. Honest rates for world-class craftsmanship.
