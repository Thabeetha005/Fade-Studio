GUEST CONCIERGE

Studio Etiquette.

To preserve a peaceful, unhurried atmosphere for all guests, please keep the following guidelines in mind.

01

Arrival Time

Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes prior to your booking. Relax in our waiting lounge with our curated print archives and espresso bar.

02

Rescheduling

Plans change. If you must adjust your reservation, simply call or WhatsApp our front desk at least 2 hours in advance so another guest may claim the chair.

03

Calm Environment

We maintain an acoustic environment with soft ambient audio. We kindly ask guests to keep mobile calls brief and silent on speaker.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions.

Do you accept walk-in clients?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcomed based on stylist availability. However, weekends and evenings between 5:00 PM and 8:30 PM fill rapidly, so an online booking guarantees zero wait time.

How long does a haircut session take?

A classic haircut takes roughly 40 minutes. Skin fades and beard combinations take 50 to 60 minutes, ensuring careful blade detailing and straight-razor finishes.
