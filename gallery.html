QUALITY BENCHMARK

Our standard of consistency.

Every photograph in our gallery reflects actual client chair sessions — never synthetic over-filtered photos.

01

True Natural Light

Our cutting stations feature 5000K daylight-balanced illumination so your haircut looks the same under natural sun as it does in our chair.

02

Sanitized Steel

All straight-edge razors, guards, and shears undergo medical-grade ultrasonic cleansing and UV sterilization between every guest.

03

Wearability

A great haircut must grow out gracefully. We cut with internal weight distribution so your hair maintains its shape 3 to 4 weeks later.
