DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy.

At FADE STUDIO, your privacy is treated with the same meticulous care as your hair. This document explains how we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal information.

01. Information We Collect

When you schedule an appointment, subscribe to our offers, or submit an inquiry at FADE STUDIO, we collect details necessary to serve you well. This includes your full name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment date and time, and any optional styling notes or hair history you choose to provide.

In-studio, our stylists may record notes regarding your clipper guard sizes, hair colour formulas, or specific scalp sensitivities to ensure consistent results across all your future visits.

02. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to deliver our grooming services. This includes transmitting appointment confirmation SMS messages, reminder notifications 24 hours prior to your scheduled chair time, and verifying your eligibility for seasonal promotions and first-visit vouchers.

We do not sell, lease, rent, or trade guest personal information to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or corporate brokers.

03. Digital Communications & Offers

If you voluntarily enter your email to claim our 15% first-visit discount or seasonal privileges, we will occasionally send you studio news, new service introductions, and exclusive guest invitations.

You retain the right to opt out of promotional communications at any point by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by notifying our concierge desk at concierge@fadestudio.in.

04. Data Protection & Security

We enforce modern technical and organizational safeguards to shield your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Our digital appointment registers and online forms communicate over encrypted SSL/TLS channels.

Access to client records is restricted strictly to authorized studio receptionists and management staff on a need-to-know basis.

05. Cookies & Local Analytics

Our website uses minimal, functional browser cookies to facilitate session navigation, maintain your preferred booking parameters, and ensure smooth script execution. We do not track you across unrelated third-party websites or deploy aggressive profiling cookies.

06. Your Rights & Data Access

Under applicable Indian data privacy regulations and ethical salon governance, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to out-of-date phone numbers or names, or request complete deletion of your guest profile from our system.

For any privacy inquiries or data requests, please write to our Data Officer at privacy@fadestudio.in or visit our Indiranagar studio in person.

GUEST CONFIDENTIALITY
