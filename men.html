THE FADE STUDIO EXPERIENCE

What to expect on your first visit.

We never rush the chair. Our disciplined 3-step service flow ensures you leave looking and feeling your absolute best.

01

Consult

We inspect your crown growth patterns, hairline contours, and jaw angles. We discuss how you wear your hair and recommend a length and fade taper that works seamlessly.

02

Cut

Crafted with precision sectioning, Japanese steel shear work, and cordless zero-gap clippers. We detail every millimeter, fading gradually from skin to weight.

03

Finish

Straight-razor neck clean, refreshing tonic splash, blow-dry sculpting, and high-performance matte clay or sea salt spray. You leave sharp, clean, and styled.
